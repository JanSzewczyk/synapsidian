The work side of the morning brief. The core's morning run calls it with `morning`; you can also ask for it alone ("what PRs need me?", "my open tickets").

```text
/synapsidian-work:work-brief [morning]
```

## What it reads

| Source | How |
| --- | --- |
| Pull requests | `synapsidian-work prs` over every repository in `github_repos`: waiting on you, ready to merge, stuck with the reason, in review, drafts, to escalate, and your PRs merged since the done window |
| Jira | your open tickets in `jira_projects`, through the Atlassian connector |
| Email | threads from the last two days that ask you for something you have not answered |
| Slack | mentions and DMs from the last two days that end in a question or ask you have not answered or reacted to |

A source that is not configured or not available is skipped silently. Everything is read-only.

## What it files

Unanswered asks from email and Slack become tasks, with `source: email` or `slack` and a link to the thread (D64):

* only asks addressed to you that you have not answered or acknowledged; not newsletters, notifications, FYIs or asks to someone else;
* deduplicated by link, against open tasks by meaning, and against tasks closed in the last 7 days;
* at most 5 per run, titled in plain words, with a `due` only when the message states a date;
* money, health and credentials are never filed.

It only creates tasks: it never edits, closes or re-dates one you have.

<Aside title="Pull requests are never tasks">
  No run creates a task, checklist line or note for a pull request, not even a review waiting on you (D96). The brief lists them, and that is all.
</Aside>

## The reply

1. **Needs attention:** PRs to escalate, and asks not filed as a task.
2. **Done:** PRs merged since the done window.
3. **Pull requests:** a tally of every group, then *To review*, *Ready to merge*, *Stuck* (with the reason), *In review* and *Drafts*, each with its count; then **Jira**, at most 6 tickets.
4. **Files** it changed, for the morning commit.

With `morning`, the morning run places these parts in the brief and commits. Without it, the skill commits itself and replies with parts 1 to 3.
