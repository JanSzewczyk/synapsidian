Turns the rough topics of an upcoming 1:1 into a proper prep, for a 1:1 or a difficult conversation with your manager, a report or a peer. It is interactive by design: the value is in your answers, so it never guesses one to skip a question.

```text
/synapsidian-work:one-to-one-triage <person or 1:1 note>
```

## Steps

1. **The note and the ground rules.** It finds the upcoming 1:1 note, asking when it is unclear whose; with none, it offers [`one-to-one prepare`](/synapsidian/0.34/extensions/work/one-to-one/) first. Then one round of questions: who is on the other side, anything off-limits, when it is and how much time there is, the tone, and topics to skip, merge or take first.
2. **One topic at a time**, in the note's order. It does not move on until a topic is closed:
   * restates the goal as an outcome and checks it;
   * surfaces the history: earlier mentions in past 1:1s and the vault, commitments, earlier versions of the ask;
   * asks specific questions about the problem, the change and the evidence;
   * researches only when you ask;
   * compares the options in a table when two or more are weighed;
   * closes with Goal, Context, Problem, Proposed change and Supporting arguments.
3. **The script, last.** For each topic, in English: a soft opening, the ask framed tentatively ("would it make sense to…"), every solid argument, and an open question at the end. It presents an option and never pushes: no "we need to", no ultimatums. Critical topics go softer still, and you are told.
4. **Written back** into the same note, under each topic: the sections above, the comparison table, and the script as a `> [!quote] Script` callout. Your wording of the topic is kept. It commits and replies with the note's link.

Everyone named is treated professionally: situations and proposals, never character judgements. Nothing is sent to anyone.
