On the day of a 1:1 you open one note and see the two to four things you want to say, each written out. The shape stays the same every week.

```text
/synapsidian-work:one-to-one prepare <person>
/synapsidian-work:one-to-one add <person> <topic>
/synapsidian-work:one-to-one work <person> <topic>
/synapsidian-work:one-to-one tidy <person>
/synapsidian-work:one-to-one read <person>
```

Claude also picks it whenever a one-on-one comes up ("add a topic for my next 1:1 with Alex").

## Where things live

| Note | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `People/<Name>.md` | `## Open topics`, the backlog with no cap; `## History`, links to past meetings |
| `Meetings/<date> 1-1 <Name>.md` | `## Talking points` (2 to 4 topics), `## Notes`, `## Action items` (checkboxes) |

A topic is a `###` heading of at most 10 words, with its body in prose: the situation, the words to say, the ask, questions, links. Notes are in English, whatever language you write in.

The meeting is found in the calendar, today to 21 days ahead: an event with exactly two attendees, you and that person. With none, the topic goes to the person's Open topics, and no date is invented.

## Modes

* **prepare.** Finds or creates the note, one per date. Talking points come from the person's Open topics (a topic moves with its body), unfinished business from the previous 1:1, and decisions that never landed. Every unticked action item from the previous 1:1 is copied as `- [ ] <item> (from YYYY-MM-DD)`; past notes are never edited. With your manager (`manager` in About.md), it lists your [wins](/synapsidian/0.34/extensions/work/win/) since the last 1:1 in the reply and offers to make them a topic.
* **add.** Acts at once and confirms in one line: a new topic on the next 1:1 note, or a line in Open topics when no meeting is scheduled. At four topics it does not add a fifth; it says which one it would move out and lets you pick.
* **work.** Files what you say under the right topic, keeping your phrasing. Asked for a draft, it writes one in the first person and says it is a draft. For a hard topic it suggests [`one-to-one-triage`](/synapsidian/0.34/extensions/work/one-to-one-triage/).
* **tidy.** Fixes structure, never content: sections named and ordered, every topic a heading, duplicates merged, action items as checkboxes. Asked whether you are ready, it reports topics with no body, topics with no ask, and topics carried three weeks without discussion.
* **read.** The headings and meeting time, one topic in full, or the previous note.

## Rules

* At most one question, only when acting would guess at something only you know.
* Nobody's verbatim message goes into a note; your words, or a short quote where the wording matters.
* Compensation, health and other people's private situations stay a topic in your words, with no figures or detail.
* The calendar is only read; nothing is sent to anyone.
