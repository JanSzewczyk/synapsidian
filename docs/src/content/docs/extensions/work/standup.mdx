title: "/synapsidian-work:standup"
description: The stand-up note - what you did, what you are on, any blocker - in spoken English.
---

Prepares what you say at the stand-up, from the vault and your work sources, and saves it to today's daily note. A scheduled task runs it an hour before the stand-up on your `standup_days`.

```text
/synapsidian-work:standup
```

## The window

`synapsidian-work standup window` gives the time of the previous stand-up from `standup_days` and `standup_time`. Every query uses that timestamp, not just a date.

## Sources

| Source | What |
| --- | --- |
| Devlogs | the `devlog` block of each daily note since then |
| Today's sessions | `synapsidian sessions digest --since … --scan`, sessions still open included |
| Jira | your tickets updated since then, and those in progress |
| GitHub | your PRs updated since then, and those you are involved in |
| Git | your own commits in `work_repo_paths`, by each repository's `user.email` |
| Calendar | working sessions such as planning or a design review; not routine calls or the stand-up itself |

A ticket, its PR and its commits are one piece of work: one bullet, under the ticket key, at its furthest state.

## The note

```markdown
- ABC-1312 — I've been working on the data export task; the PR is up now, just waiting on a review.
- ABC-1298 — I finished off the reporting one, and that's been merged since Tuesday.
- Today — I'm carrying on with the CSV import, and I'd like to get a PR open by the end of the day.
- No blockers.
```

- Up to 5 bullets of what was done, oldest first, led by the ticket key or PR number.
- One `Today —` bullet, then one `Blocker —` bullet or `No blockers.` A blocker is never invented.
- One or two spoken sentences each, first person: what the work concerns, never what changed in the code, and always where it landed.

It is saved as the `standup` block under `## Stand-up` in today's note, committed, and shown to you exactly as saved.

Everything outside the vault is read-only: no ticket is touched, no PR reviewed, nothing sent.
