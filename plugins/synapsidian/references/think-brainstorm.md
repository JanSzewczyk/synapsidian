# think: brainstorm

Work a problem out with the user, one step at a time, ending in named options and one recommendation.

1. Restate the problem in one sentence and check the vault for prior thinking (`synapsidian find`, `--type idea`, `--type decision`, earlier `thinking` notes).
2. **One question per turn.** Ask the single question whose answer narrows the space most (constraints, goal, who it is for, what was tried). Use AskUserQuestion when 2-4 concrete answers are plausible. Stop asking when you could name the options; three to five questions is typical.
3. **Name the options**: 3-5 genuinely different approaches, each with a short name, what it is, its main upside, its main cost. At least one should be unconventional; one may be "do nothing".
4. **Recommend one**, with the reason and the first concrete step. Say what you'd need to learn to be more sure.
5. Write a `thinking` note with `kind=brainstorm` (Question, Options, Recommendation, How we got there: the questions asked and the answers, briefly). If the user picks an option to pursue, offer `/synapsidian:think graduate` (for an idea) or a task.
6. In chat: the options as a short list and the recommendation.
